## Facts
- **restrooms:** Restrooms: The restrooms are toward the back of the bar — head past the bar and up the steps to the deck. You'll find them behind the wall on the right.
- **parking:** Parking: The gravel lot next to Lucky Duck is shared — you're welcome to park there when you visit Clara's. Street parking on Cesar Chavez works too.

## Menu highlights
- Clara's Spritz ($11): Aperol, prosecco, a twist of grapefruit oil.
- Limoncello Fizz ($12): Limoncello, soda, lemon, a little thyme.
- Frozen Negroni ($13): Campari, gin, sweet vermouth — slushied.
- Frosé ($12): Rosé, strawberry, lemon. Dangerously easy.
- Coupe Martini ($14): Gin or vodka, dry, lemon or olive.
- Penguin Punch ($9): Frozen, very pink, all the fun, none of the buzz.

## FAQ
Q: What is Clara's Day Dive?
A: Clara's Day Dive is a neighborhood coupe bar and patio in East Austin, Texas. We serve day drinks, spritzes, frozen cocktails, and zero-proof options in a warm, retro-playful setting with rotating food trucks and morning coffee.

Q: Where is Clara's Day Dive located?
A: 1814 E Cesar Chavez St, Austin, TX 78702 — on the east side, with a shaded dog-friendly patio open late.

Q: What are Clara's Day Dive hours?
A: Monday through Thursday 3pm to midnight; Friday noon to 2am; Saturday and Sunday 11am to 2am.

Q: Does Clara's Day Dive serve food?
A: We host rotating food trucks — Bird Bird Biscuit on weekdays and Veracruz tacos on weekends — plus morning coffee until noon.

Q: Can I book Clara's Day Dive for a private event?
A: Yes. We host birthdays, send-offs, and patio gatherings. Reach out to our event coordinator by email or phone — details are on the Book the bar section of our website.

Q: What drinks is Clara's Day Dive known for?
A: House favorites include Clara's Spritz (Aperol, prosecco, grapefruit), frozen Frosé, the Penguin Punch zero-proof slush, and seasonal frozen Negronis.
