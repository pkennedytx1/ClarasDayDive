Cookie Policy

Last updated July 5, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember preferences and understand how visitors use pages.

How we use cookies

Our site uses a minimal set of technologies:

  • Essential cookies from our hosting provider required for basic site delivery and security.
  • Self-hosted web fonts — typography files served from our domain, not from Google.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking on this site today.

Third-party services

Instagram and food-truck partner links are plain links, not embeds — they do not set cookies on our site. Ask Clara sends your message to our AWS API; see our Privacy Policy for details.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some parts of the site work.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our site changes. See the date at the top of this page for the latest version.
