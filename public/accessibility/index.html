Accessibility Statement

Last updated July 5, 2026

Our commitment

Clara's Day Dive is committed to making our website and venue welcoming to as many guests as possible. We aim to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines for our digital presence and provide reasonable accommodations at our bar and patio.

Website accessibility

We designed this site mobile-first with accessibility in mind, including:

  • Semantic HTML structure and logical heading order.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and visible focus indicators.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Sufficient color contrast on primary text and interactive elements.
  • Skip navigation link to main content.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion settings.

Known limitations

Some brand illustrations are decorative or artistic and may not convey information in text form. Our custom wordmark is rendered as images in places; we provide text alternatives where the logo carries meaning.

We are actively improving accessibility. If you encounter a barrier on our site, please tell us — we want to fix it.

Physical accessibility

Our patio is ground-level with shaded seating. If you have questions about entry, seating, or accommodations for a visit or private event, contact us before you arrive and we will do our best to help.

Feedback and assistance

We welcome accessibility feedback. Contact us using the email or phone in our Contact section. Please include the page URL or describe the issue you experienced. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Third-party content

Links to external services (such as Instagram) are governed by those providers' accessibility practices.
